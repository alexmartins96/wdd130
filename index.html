Utah

Utah is an amazing state, smaller in size compared to many well-known states, but offering a high quality of life and excellent public safety. Approximately 42% of the population are members of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints. The state is renowned for its world-class winter sports, including snowboarding and skiing, as well as its stunning national parks and natural landscapes.
